## 0. Ruling

The universal stock-discovery scientific unit is no longer a complete company ontology, a rerating story, or a bottleneck graph. It is the **smallest point-in-time representation sufficient to predict one explicit future payoff state or distribution**.

```text
Find PIT states with a favorable forward payoff distribution.

Opportunity
= EligibleQuality
× Edge
× Asymmetry
× Capturability
```

Interpretation:

- `EligibleQuality` is any small lawful representation that may support a favorable future payoff distribution: quality/compounding, fundamental transition, mechanical advantage, statistical continuation, or another separately frozen kernel.
- `Edge` is the difference between the future state implied by the kernel and the state currently reflected in observable market behavior/pricing.
- `Asymmetry` asks how much is earned when right, how much is lost when partly wrong, and how severe catastrophic failure is.
- `Capturability` covers recognition, entry, liquidity, holding horizon, costs, and exit.

A mechanistic graph remains legal for a mechanistic kernel. It is not a universal constitutional requirement for every stock Alpha hypothesis.

## 3. Four frozen laws

### 3.1 Complexity law

```text
Complexity must be earned marginally.
```

For a frozen kernel `I` and proposed addition `X`, `X` is retained only after a later lawful test establishes material marginal value of `I+X` versus `I`. A large feature set may not be introduced merely because its variables are economically plausible.

### 3.2 Entry-mode law

`CONTINUATION` is the first incumbent entry mode. Market recognition may therefore be tested as an additional observable source of information.

`DISLOCATION` is not part of the first empirical basis. A future dislocation kernel must separately freeze at minimum:

```text

identifiable shock
thesis-intact criterion
price damage > fundamental damage criterion
conservative downside floor
explicit hard falsifier
```

It may not borrow the first continuation basis result as proof.

### 3.3 Capturability / execution-feasibility law

Capturability is economically part of Opportunity but is **not** a universal Alpha feature surface.

For action-bearing economic freezes / D9 / promotion / capital-policy preflight / PAPER action proposals, bind `docs/architecture/execution_feasibility_v1.json`:

```text
Opportunity / Alpha → DESIRED exposure
Capturability model → feasible economic transform
CRO hard gates      → ALLOWABLE exposure
Portfolio policy    → ACTUAL target
Broker              → realized fills / account state
```

Hard risk remains hard:

```text
G(s,a) = ALLOW | BLOCK | UNRESOLVED
BLOCK = infeasible
```

No expected-return objective or lambda penalty may compensate for `BLOCK`.

Risk margins stay a **vector** (`var_margin`, `sector_margin`, `single_name_margin`, `vix_margin`, plus reason-code-specific margins). `capturability_state` is summary only and never replaces the underlying vector.

Historical execution telemetry may calibrate soft IS/slippage/latency/fill/capacity envelopes only when it is **policy-conditioned** by at least policy ID, order type/TIF, participation/sizing regime and market regime/conditioning state. Realized fills are not universal OpportunityKernel features and may not rewrite Alpha evidence.

### 3.4 Asymmetry law

No kernel may be judged by Recall/Lift alone. `AsymmetryEvaluationV1` must jointly report upside density, downside burden, false-winner burden, recognition timing, coverage/abstention, distribution tails, capital-time, turnover, and cost.

The central question is:

```text
Does the kernel improve upside density without buying unacceptable downside?
```

## 7. Q arm — exact representation

Q is **fundamental-only**. It does not include price/trend/volume or Rule100 technical quality.

The four approved fundamental group primitives are reused from the existing Rule100 v1.1 vocabulary:

```text
demand             = z_demand
inventory_supply   = z_inventory_quality_proxy
moat_pricing       = z_moat
capital_discipline = capital_cycle_score
```

For decision date `t`:

1. each primitive must be computed only from historical PIT fundamentals whose exact vintage/source bytes were available at the knowledge cut;
2. for each primitive independently, compute the average-tie percentile rank across `D_t` names with that primitive lawfully observed;
3. `q_present_count` is the number of observed primitive ranks;
4. Q is observable only when `q_present_count >= 3`;
5. `q_score` is the arithmetic mean of the observed primitive percentile ranks;
6. no missing primitive is filled with zero, cross-sectional median, or a neutral `0.5` for this basis experiment.

Thus:

```text
q_score(i,t)
= mean( pct_rank_t(g_j(i,t)) for observed j )
  if observed_group_count >= 3
else ABSTAIN
```

The Q arm selects the top `K_t` observable names by `q_score`. If fewer than `K_t` Q-observable names exist, that decision date is `Q_COVERAGE_FAILURE`; K is not shrunk.

This is a `QUALITY / COMPOUNDING` basis probe. It does not claim that historical Rule100 is proven Alpha.

## 8. M arm — exact representation

M is one variable only: trailing 60-observed-session primary-security total return ending on decision date `t`.

```text
m_raw(i,t)
= product_{s=t-59 ... t}(1 + r_i,s) - 1
```

Requirements:

```text
exactly 60 observed sessions on the same admitted security/listing identity
all return observations available by the decision cut
no benchmark residualization
no sector neutralization
no volatility normalization
no breakout/volume gate
no fitted coefficient or threshold
```

`m_score` is the average-tie date-local percentile rank of `m_raw` across M-observable names in `D_t`.

The M arm selects the top `K_t` observable names by `m_score`. If fewer than `K_t` M-observable names exist, that decision date is `M_COVERAGE_FAILURE`; K is not shrunk.

This identity is a standalone `CONTINUATION` kernel. It is not `VSB_CONFIRMATION_v1` and does not modify VSB.

## 9. Q+M arm — exact composition

Q+M is observable only when both Q and M are observable.

```text
qm_score(i,t) = q_score(i,t) * m_score(i,t)
```

No learned weights, additive coefficient, interaction search, threshold, sector adjustment, or nonlinear rescue is permitted in v1.

The Q+M arm selects the top `K_t` names by `qm_score`. If fewer than `K_t` jointly observable names exist, that date is `QM_COVERAGE_FAILURE`; K is not shrunk.

The product form is deliberately literal: both quality and continuation must be present for the composed arm to rank highly. Whether that seam adds value is the empirical question; it is not assumed by the constitution.
